What this can and can't tell you. This is a lite mix model: it finds the spend-results relationship in your history. It needs spend that actually varies (a channel spent flat every week can't be measured), 6+ months of data, and it reads correlation — a channel that only runs during your peak season will look better than it is. Channels that overlap heavily (brand search vs. Meta retargeting) can blur into each other. Use it for direction and budget splits, then verify with the Audit console and holdout tests, not as courtroom proof.
PULSE//MMM runs entirely in this page — adstock + saturation + ordinary least squares, computed in your browser. Open source (MIT): github.com/riz1k9/pulse-audit
